Privacy Policy

Effective: November 5, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how JARVIS Content Studio handles information when creators use its video preparation and TikTok draft-upload features.

2. Information processed

The Service may process TikTok authorization information and account identifiers made available through authorized TikTok APIs; creator-provided prompts, scripts, captions, and media; generated video files; upload status and technical error logs; and app settings needed to operate requested features.

3. Purposes

Information is used to authenticate the creator, create and display video drafts, perform uploads expressly approved by the creator, show upload status, maintain security, diagnose errors, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Creator control

The Service does not upload a video until the creator requests or confirms the upload. Captions and generated content remain reviewable before publication. TikTok authorization can be revoked through applicable TikTok account settings.

5. Sharing

Information is sent to TikTok only when necessary to provide an authorized TikTok feature. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or the Service, or to service providers that process information under appropriate safeguards. Personal information is not sold.

6. Storage and retention

Generated drafts and app settings may be stored on the creator's device. Authorization data and technical records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the requested functionality, security, troubleshooting, or legal compliance, and are deleted or de-identified when no longer needed.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information. No system is completely secure, and creators should protect their devices and never disclose client secrets or access tokens.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under the age required to use TikTok or to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction.

9. Requests and deletion

Creators may request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with the Service, subject to applicable law. Locally stored drafts can be deleted directly from the creator's device.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect product, legal, or security changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

11. Contact

Privacy questions and requests may be submitted through GitHub Support Issues.
