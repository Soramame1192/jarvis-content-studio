CREATOR-CONTROLLED VIDEO WORKFLOW

Create vertical video drafts with a clear review step.

JARVIS Content Studio helps creators prepare original vertical videos, captions, and upload-ready drafts. The creator reviews every item before it is sent to TikTok.

The service does not auto-follow, auto-like, auto-comment, or manipulate engagement.

Original drafts

Generate scripts, narration, captions, and 9:16 MP4 drafts for creator-owned or properly licensed material.

Human review

Creators check facts, rights, captions, and video content before approving any upload.

TikTok draft upload

With creator authorization, the app uses TikTok's Content Posting API to upload a video as a draft for completion in TikTok.

How it works

  1. The creator chooses a content category.
  2. The app creates an editable video draft and caption.
  3. The creator reviews content and confirms upload.
  4. The app sends the approved video to TikTok as a draft.
  5. The creator completes the post inside TikTok.
